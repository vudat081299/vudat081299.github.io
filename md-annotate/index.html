      Layout
      
          
        
      
        
      
          
      Changes apply as you drag and are saved in this browser.
      
          
      
            
      
            
            
      Auto = 70% of the screen, at least 1280px. Split needs about 420px per pane.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      
            
            
      45–75 characters per line reads best for long text. Off = fill the pane.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      
            
          
      
          
      
            
      
            
            
      Dims everything outside the section under the pointer: from the nearest heading above to the
              next heading. Mouse or trackpad only.
      
          
      
          
      
        
      
      
      
          

    
      
      
        
      
          
      Comment hints
      
          
        
      
        
      
          
      Ranked by how often you use them in this browser. Share = (uses + 1) / (total uses + number of hints),
            shown after 5 comments. A comment you type twice or more becomes a hint.
