          Settings
          
          
          
        
          
        
          
          
          Changes apply as you drag and are saved in this browser.
          
          
          
            
          
            
            
          Auto = 70% of the screen, at least 1280px. Split needs about 420px per pane.
          
          
          
          
          
            
          
            
            
          45–75 characters per line reads best for long text. Off = fill the pane.
          
          
          
          
          
            
          
            
          
          
          
          
            
          
            
          Scrolling one pane scrolls the other so the same passage sits in the middle of both.
          
          
          
          
          
            
          
            
          
              
              
            
          
            
          On a touch screen Focus follows the reading band.
          
            
            
          A section runs from a heading to the next heading. Follow pointer: the section under the mouse.
              Reading band: the first section between 25% and 50% of the pane's height, updated as you scroll. In Split
              the matching section in the other pane stays bright too.
          
          
          
          
          
            
          
            
            
          Fades the blocks you marked reviewed, for a top-to-bottom pass. Same switch as Dim reviewed in the Review tab.
          
          
          
          
          
        
          
      
          
              

    
    
      
          
        
          
          
          Shortcuts and gestures
          
          
        
          
        
          
          
          Recently added keys, clicks and taps.
          
          
          On a touch screen: long-press a line number to pick lines.
          
          
          
        
          
      
          
              

    
      
          
        
          
          
          Comment hints
          
          
        
          
        
          
          
          Ranked by how often you use them in this browser. Share = (uses + 1) / (total uses + number of hints),
            shown after 5 comments. A comment you type twice or more becomes a hint.
