      Data Science Step Planner

      Plan a data project as steps and sub-steps. Track status and due dates, run tasks in parallel, reopen a step when you need another iteration, and keep a log of why you decided things.

      Made for data projects, works for any project.

      Templates

      • Built on published methods such as CRISP-DM 1.0 and PPDAC, with notes on common traps.
      • Pick one when you create a project, or start blank.
      • Rename, add or remove anything afterwards.

      Your data

      • Saved only in this browser. No account, nothing is uploaded.
      • Export Markdown to share or read on GitHub.
      • Back up as JSON to keep it or move it to another device.

      Keyboard

      • Shift + click a chip: any status
      • Alt + arrows on a title: move
      • Ctrl / ⌘ + Z: undo
      A static page: no server, no tracking. All pages Source
